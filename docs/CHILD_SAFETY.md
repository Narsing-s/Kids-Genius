# Child safety and privacy baseline

- The parent is the account holder; children use parent-created profiles.
- Prefer a nickname and age band. Avoid collecting a child's full name, exact birth date, address, school, contacts, photos or precise location.
- Do not add public profiles, direct messaging, open user-generated content or behavioural advertising.
- Parent consent, privacy notices, deletion/export workflows and a lawful basis must be implemented and reviewed before launch.
- Apply least-privilege database access and test Row Level Security with two unrelated parent accounts.
- Do not expose service-role keys in browser or mobile bundles.
- Keep ages 1–2 parent-assisted, untimed and non-competitive.
- Use accessible text, audio controls, reduced-motion support and age-appropriate feedback.
- Obtain qualified legal review for applicable child privacy requirements, including India's DPDP framework and any other launch jurisdiction.
